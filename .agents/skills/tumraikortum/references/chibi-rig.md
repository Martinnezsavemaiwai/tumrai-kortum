# Skeleton rig (`kit/rig.js`) — chibi, explainer, brick, heroic, round

`R.draw(ctx, { x, y, s, body, who, t, ...R.act(t, keys) })` draws one figure, feet at (x, y), 1000 px tall at
`s: 1`. One skeleton for every body, so poses and acting carry across:

| body | look | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `chibi` | big head, but a neck, elbows and 5-finger hands; the default body | most genres: drama, comedy, romance, cooking. Any style |
| `explainer` | ~1:4.5, round head, small dot eyes, soft one-light shading, optional `glasses` (frame colour) and `jacket` | สรุปหนังสือ / explainer shorts; `flat`. Pairs with `X.povHands` |
| `brick` | toy brick figure: cylinder head with a stud, printed dot-eye face, moulded hair piece, trapezoid torso with the outfit printed on (`jacket`, `vest`, `collar`, `tie`, `bow`, `mark`), block legs, C-shaped hands | toys, kids, "build" formats; pair with STYLE `bricks`. Hands are always the C grip (`handL`/`handR` ignored), knees only shorten the legs, `shorts`/`apron`/`sash`/`belt`/`band` are not drawn |
| `heroic` | ~1:7.5 adult: small head, broad shoulders, narrow waist, long legs | action, sports, grown-up drama. Hand-to-face poses (`think`, `peace`, `scared`) are tuned to chibi arms and land beside the head here — use `reachR` to the chin/cheek when the hand must touch the face |
| `round` | a ball torso from the shoulders to below the hips, short arms and legs | mascots, kids, elders. Trousers/skirts follow the ball. Short arms can't reach the face: hand-to-face poses point toward it |

- `who`: a `R.CAST` key or an object `{ body, skin, hair, hairStyle: 'long'|'short'|'bob'|'ponytail'|'twintail'|'spiky'|'bald'|'tuft'|'buzz'|'twoblock'|'comma'|'sidepart'|'swept'|'bangs'|'braids'|'bun'|'curly', eye, top, collar, tie, bow, jacket, vest, glasses, accent (hair ties), bottom, bottomStyle: 'pants'|'skirt'|'shorts'|'sarong', shoes, shoeStyle: 'sneaker'|'leather'|'loafer'|'boot'|'sandal'|'flipflop', old, … }`. `R.check(who)` lists every problem (unknown field, bad colour, unknown style); `R.SPEC` is the full list. `body` in the `who` is used when `R.draw` gets none.
- Hair styles by silhouette: short men's cuts `short` · `buzz` (นักเรียน/เกรียน) · `twoblock` (short clipped sides, a longer textured top and fringe, no hair behind the ears) · `comma` (centre-parted curtain with tucked tips) · `sidepart` (รองทรง, volume swept from a left part) · `swept` (spiky, rising and swept back) · `spiky` (a crown of spikes) · `tuft`; long and tied `long` · `bangs` (straight หน้าม้า, level cut) · `bob` (ติ่งหู/jaw) · `ponytail` · `twintail` · `braids` (เปียสองข้าง, in front of the shoulders) · `bun` (มวย on the crown); `curly` (ผมดัด, tight curls); `bald`. Give characters who share a scene different silhouettes, not just colours. Every style is drawn on chibi, explainer, heroic and round; on brick the hair is one moulded piece with the style's main feature (bun, braids, curls, spikes, long back, two block's clipped side panels under a raised top).
- Shoes (`shoeStyle`, colour `shoes`, optional `socks`): `sneaker` (default: light rubber sole, toe cap, laces) · `leather` (laced derby, dark welted sole, heel) · `loafer` (low slip-on, saddle strap) · `boot` (laced shaft up the shin, lug sole) · `sandal` (bare foot on a footbed, two buckled straps) · `flipflop` (bare foot, thong strap). Both feet sit under the ankles, toes turned a little out; the shin, trouser hem and socks end inside the shoe. On brick the shoe is one moulded block per leg, so sneaker / leather / loafer differ mostly by sole colour and markings — pick boot, sandal or flipflop when the shoe must read from far away.
- Joints (radians): `lSh rSh` (0 hanging, + out/up), `lEl rEl` (+ further out, − folds toward the face; ≈ −3.3 with a shoulder ≈ 1 brings the hand to the cheek), `lHip rHip lKnee rKnee`, `lean`, `tilt`, `bob`, `sx sy`.
- Face: `eyes` normal happy closed wide shock angry sad sly · `mouth` smile open o grin frown wavy smirk scream flat · `talk` 0–1 (from the voice: `Kit.talk(S.mouth, who, t)`, see `sound.md` → Lip-sync) · `look lookY` · `blink`.
- Hands: `handL handR` = open fist point peace thumb relax. `fx`: sweat blush tears anger shock sparkle.
- `R.POSES`: idle happy wave cheer point peace thumbsup shrug think scared startle sad angry sneaky talk. `R.act(t, keys, { seed, gestures, alive, move })` = anticipation dip (builds 0.12 s before a key, springs back during the move) → slow-in + overshoot move (`outBack(smoothstep)`, 0.35 s) with forearms, head and gaze trailing (`LAG`) → hold; wiggles fade in and out; eyes/mouth swap behind a blink. `alive` (default) adds breathing, blinks (~20/min) and gaze shifts from `seed` — **give every character its own seed** or they blink together. `gestures: [[t, 'jump'|'nod'|'shake'|'shiver']]` add on top (`R.GESTURES`). Returns `blink` and `sway` (hair lag) too: spread the whole result into `R.draw`. `R.idle(t, seed)` alone for characters with no keys. `R.walk(t)` adds a step cycle. Tests: `scripts/rig.test.mjs` (motion, plus the real `gfx.js` + rig drawn on a recording canvas: every body × pose draws finite geometry with its feet on the ground, eyes on the `R.faces`/`Direct.subject` eye line, wrists on reach targets, old bodies' geometry pinned); graphs/onion skin: `examples/rig-lab` pages 10–12, the four newer bodies at 18–33 s.
- A new body: a `BODY` row (positive numbers; `eye`, `eyeR`, `eyeX` place the face), drawn by `front()` or its own function, and it must keep the head where `draw()`/`Direct.subject` compute it (`hy = neckTop − head[1]·0.92`) — the tests above check that.
- Real motion for whole-body actions (`kit/moves.js`): `{ ...Moves.play(t - start, 'jump', { base: R.act(t, keys, { seed }), body }) }` instead of keying them by hand — jumping jacks, squats, a side bend, a big wave, a laugh, a scared self-hug, a crouch-and-jump, angry arms, reaching up, a stretch. It eases in from the current pose over `fade` (0.25 s) and back out, keeps the base's face and hands; `loop`/`hold` for repeats; `p.dx·s` added to x moves the figure with the hips. Bodies with short limbs (chibi) keep the timing but the shapes get smaller — check a still. New clips: `scripts/mocap.mjs <name> <CMU id> --from s --to s` (index: cmu-mocap-index-text.txt), then look at a contact sheet: walks/runs and anything toward the camera do not read from the front. Tests: `scripts/mocap.test.mjs`.
- Check a new character or pose on a model sheet (render stills, tile, look) before using it in shots — e.g. a film whose `draw` for `t < 0` lays out every pose of one body in a grid, rendered with `render.js stills -1`. `examples/longform-demo/` uses `explainer` and `chibi` bodies with `R.act` and `R.walk`.
